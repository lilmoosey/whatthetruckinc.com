WHO WE ARE

WHAT THE TRUCK INC is an active carrier & broker registered with the FMCSA since March 2026. Based in Tracy, CA, we're classified for both interstate and intrastate operations, with the ability to operate beyond 100 miles intrastate.

We specialize in Box Truck, Dry Van, and Hotshot freight — keeping goods moving efficiently throughout California and beyond. Our team is small but professional, built on reliability and straightforward service.
